# SOC Investigation Methodology

## Purpose

This document defines the investigation methodology used throughout this repository.

The objective is to investigate security alerts systematically, use evidence to validate hypotheses, determine the scope and impact of suspicious activity, and produce an evidence-based conclusion.

---

```text

       ↓

       ↓

       ↓

       ↓

       ↓
